The system's container: white, 10px radius, hairline border, sm shadow — used for rooms, venues, amenities and reservations.

```jsx
<Card image="/assets/room.jpg" interactive>
  <CardTitle>Harbour Suite</CardTitle>
  <CardMeta>King bed · Sleeps 2 · 38 m²</CardMeta>
</Card>
```

`featured` inverts the card to navy for one highlighted item per view. `arch` applies the brand archway radius — reserve it for venue/celebration content. Never add a coloured left border.
